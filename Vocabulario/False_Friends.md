# False Friends

This is to be a list of words in Spanish that resemble words in other languages, but have different meanings--sometimes even opposites.

## Spanish and English

#### *La Palabra: Embarazada*
ENG -> SPA: embarrassed = avergonzado
SPA -> ENG: embarazada = pregnant
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
It looks like embarrassed, but this is definitely incorrect; it means pregnant

#### *La Palabra: Constipado*
ENG -> SPA: constipated = estreñido
SPA -> ENG: constipado = congested/having a cold
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Both ailments, but very different

#### *La Palabra: Asistir*
ENG -> SPA: assits = ayudar
SPA -> ENG: asistir = to attend/be present
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Not the worst of these, but still could lead to confusion

#### *La Palabra: Carpeta*
ENG -> SPA: carpet = alfombra
SPA -> ENG: carpeta = folder/binder
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Though one might get a 'carpeta de alfombra' at a carpet store, best to know which is which

#### *La Palabra: Suceso*
ENG -> SPA: success = éxito
SPA -> ENG: suceso = happened
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Actually one of those cases where it is essentially the same word as in Italian--succedere = suceder--so the same 'false friend' situation here with English.

#### *La Palabra: Educado*
ENG -> SPA: educated = intruido
SPA -> ENG: educato = well behaved/polite
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Again, the same 'false friend' situation as with italian; a well know example here, but the difference in this meaning is enough to still give native speakers cause to laugh at you.

#### *La Palabra: Soportar*
ENG -> SPA: support = apoyar
SPA -> ENG: soportar = to tolerate/put up with
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Could lead to some potentially disastrous misunderstandings...

#### *La Palabra: Fábrica*
ENG -> SPA: fabric = tela
SPA -> ENG: fábrica = factory
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
One should think more along the lines of 'fabricate' for the english speaker.

#### *La Palabra: Actualmente*
ENG -> SPA: actually = en realidad/en hecho
SPA -> ENG: actualmente = currently/at present
#### *El Descripción, en Inglés:*
Related conceptually, but clearly not the same.

## Spanish and Italian

#### *La Palabra: Burro*
IT -> SPA: burro = manteca
SPA -> IT: burro = asino
#### *En Inglés:*
Burro means butter in Italiano, and donkey in Español

#### *La Palabra: Largo*
IT -> SPA: largo = ancho
SPA -> IT: largo = lungo
#### *En Inglés:*
Largo means wide in Italiano, and long in Español

#### *La Palabra: Caldo*
IT -> SPA: caldo = caliente
SPA -> IT: largo = brodo
#### *En Inglés:*
Caldo means hot in Italiano, and broth in Español

#### *La Palabra: Autista*
IT -> SPA: autista = el conductor
SPA -> IT: autista = autistico
#### *En Inglés:*
Autista means a driver/conductor in Italiano, and an autistic person in Español

#### *La Palabra: Gamba*
IT -> SPA: gamba = pierna
SPA -> IT: gamba = camarón/gambero
#### *En Inglés:*
Gamba means leg in Italiano, and shrimp/prawn in Español

#### *La Palabra: Guardar/Guardare*
IT -> SPA: guardare = mirar
SPA -> IT: guardar = mantenere/proteggere
#### *En Inglés:*
Guardare means to watch/look at in Italiano, and guardar means to guard/save/keep in Español

#### *La Palabra: Mirar/Mirare*
IT -> SPA: mirare = apuntar
SPA -> IT: mirar = guardare
#### *En Inglés:*
Mirare means to take aim/aim at in Italiano, and mirar means to watch/look at in Español

#### *La Palabra: Pronto*
IT -> SPA: pronto = presto
SPA -> IT: pronto = presto
#### *En Inglés:*
Pronto means ready in Italiano, and early in Español

#### *La Palabra: Presto*
IT -> SPA: presto = pronto
SPA -> IT: presto = pronto
#### *En Inglés:*
Presto means early in Italiano, and ready in Español
