# Pretérito Pluscuamperfecto

Also known as the antecopretérito, or sometimes the pasado perfecto, this is the *past perfect* tense. As a compound tense, it is formed similar to other perfect tenses by using a form of 'haber' as an auxiliary verb and the participle form of a verb.

This tense is used to describe an action that had occurred before another action/event in the past. It is similar to the Italian trapassato prossimo, and can be translated to English as the "I had done..." type of expression.

## Conjugations

This tense is formed by using haber in its *conditional* tense conjugation, in combination with the *past participle* of the target verb.

### *Form of Haber*

| Singular       | Ending | Plural           | Ending   |
|:---------------|-------:|:-----------------|---------:|
| Yo             | había  | Nosotros         | habíamos |
| Tu             | habías | Vosotros         | habíais  |
| Él/Ella/Usted  | había  | Ellos/as/Ustedes | habían   |

The past participles of verbs are discussed [HERE], but can generally be formed by changing the ending of the infinitive form of a verb to '-ado' for '-ar' verbs annd '-ido' for '-er' and '-ir' verbs. There are some irregulars to be aware of, and one can refer to the file mentioned previously to find a list of these.
