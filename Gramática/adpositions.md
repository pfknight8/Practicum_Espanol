# Adpositions

Spanish, like English, utilizes primarily prepositions (las preposiciones) and prepositional phrases. 

## Prepositions

| Español    | English          | Notes |
|:----------:|:----------------:|:------|
| En         | in, on, at       ||
| A          | to               ||
| De         | of, from         ||
| Hacia      | towards          ||
| Hasta      | until, as far as ||
| Para       | for              | Used for destinations, recipients, deadlines, purpose |
| Por        | for              | Used for cause, duration, exchange (paid _ for _), means of transportation/communication |
| Con        | with             ||
| Sin        | without          ||
| Desde      | since, from      ||
| Durante    | during           ||
| bajo       | below, under     ||
| ante       | before           ||
| entre      | between          ||
| contra     | against          ||
| sobre      | on top of, about | when 'about' is regarding a topic--ie "on" a topic; can approximate time, as well |
| tras       | after, behind    ||
| según      | according to     ||


## Compound Prepositions

| Español          | English                  | Notes |
|:----------------:|:------------------------:|:------|
| además de        | as well as, besides      ||
| al lado de       | beside (physically)      ||
| alrededor de     | around, surrounding      | physically surrounding; also approximating numbers and time |
| antes de         | before                   | Time and order of actions |
| delante de       | before, in front of      | Location and place; phyisically in "front of" |
| cerca de         | near                     ||
| lejos de         | far from                 ||
| debajo de        | underneath               ||
| dentro de        | inside                   ||
| fuera de         | outside, out of          ||
| encima de        | on top of                | will be on the very top, or topmost of a stack, where applicable |
| junto a          | next to, together with   ||
| (en) frente a/de | opposite, facing towards ||
